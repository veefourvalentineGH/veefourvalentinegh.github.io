Meet Vee

A voice for the quiet hours.

Raised in Chicago and trained as a vocalist from her pre-teens, Vee Valentine sings easy-listening pop shaped by classic jazz and sophisticated lounge standards. Her sound is smooth, intimate, and quietly addictive: candlelit rooms, late conversations, melodies that stay with you.

Now she is stepping into her own voice as a songwriter.
