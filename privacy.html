Privacy

Privacy notice

This site is built to collect as little as possible.

No tracking. veevalentine.com uses no analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies, and it loads no scripts, fonts, or media from other companies.

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the domain and email forwarding run through Cloudflare. Like any host, they may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies.

Email. If you write to contact@veevalentine.com or ask to join the list, we receive your email address and message. We use them to reply and, if you asked to join the list, to send Vee’s news. We don’t sell or share them. To be removed, email us and we’ll delete your address.

Questions? Write to contact@veevalentine.com.
